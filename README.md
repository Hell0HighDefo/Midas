## Crafting
- Can be crafted by surrounding any mob head with 8 gold, similarly to Golden Apples

![A player crafting a Golden Head by surrounding a player head with 8 gold ingots](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Hell0HighDefo/Midas/a9450c4285663d3e935ebba9b3a5de5173cf56a0/.webassets/golden_head_crafting.gif)


## Compatibility 
**[Enchancement](https://modrinth.com/mod/enchancement)** is recommended to use alongside **Midas** to ease the collection of mob heads, try getting **Beheading** on an axe. 
In a similar vein, you may also install VanillaTweaks' [Player Head Drops](https://vanillatweaks.net/share#WHFcOd) to make player heads obtainable in Vanilla, but balancing may be a bit messy.

I would not recommend using VanillaTweaks' **[More Mob Heads](https://vanillatweaks.net/share#k3V2Ai)**, as the pack uses modified player heads, and would most likely conflict with balancing.
